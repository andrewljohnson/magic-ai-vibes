Deck scouting

Win rates: each bot pilots the deck against the other ten decks flown by Handcrafted. Notes are written from step-by-step replay review of Handcrafted-vs-SPZ games in each deck (four games per deck, rotating opponents) — click a replay to walk the actual game.

Method: 44 scouted games (night-d*) generated at fixed seeds (build/replay), reviewed as full action logs. Matrix data from the latest matchup-matrix run. Descriptions cover the current champion; they are re-earned, not carried forward, after each promotion.
